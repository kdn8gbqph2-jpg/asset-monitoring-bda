Bharatpur Development Authority — Online Services

भरतपुर विकास प्राधिकरण (BDA) — सभी ऑनलाइन सेवाएँ एक जगह

A single gateway to all BDA online portals — pump monitoring, document archives, engineering works and accounts.

The Bharatpur Development Authority (BDA) — भरतपुर विकास प्राधिकरण — is the urban development body for Bharatpur, Rajasthan. This page is the official gateway to BDA's online services: monsoon drainage pump monitoring, the document archive (DMS), the engineering works (E-Works) portal, and accounts (E-Accounts). Select a service below to open its portal. For department information and notices, visit the official BDA website on rajasthan.gov.in.

भरतपुर विकास प्राधिकरण (बीडीए) भरतपुर, राजस्थान का नगर विकास निकाय है। यह पृष्ठ बीडीए की सभी ऑनलाइन सेवाओं — पंप (जल निकासी) निगरानी, दस्तावेज़ संग्रह, अभियांत्रिकी कार्य, और लेखा — का एकल आधिकारिक प्रवेश-द्वार है।

Pump Management System (PMS)

Real-time monitoring of Bharatpur's monsoon drainage pumps with operator-logged on/off status, running hours and history. Staff sign in to log and review pump activity.

भरतपुर के मानसून जल निकासी पंपों की वास्तविक-समय निगरानी, चालू/बंद स्थिति और रनिंग आवर्स के साथ।

pms.bdabharatpur.org Open

Document Management System (DMS)

Digital archive of BDA records, files and scanned documents. Sign in to search, view and download departmental records.

बीडीए रिकॉर्ड, फाइलों और स्कैन किए गए दस्तावेज़ों का डिजिटल संग्रह।

archives-bda.cloud Open

E-Works — Engineering Works

BDA engineering works portal for project tracking, tenders and work orders in Bharatpur.

परियोजना ट्रैकिंग, निविदाओं और कार्य आदेशों के लिए बीडीए अभियांत्रिकी कार्य पोर्टल।

e-nagar.com Open

E-Accounts — Accounts & Finance

Accounts and financial-management portal for BDA operations, payments and ledgers.

बीडीए संचालन, भुगतान और बहीखातों के लिए लेखा एवं वित्तीय प्रबंधन पोर्टल।

e-nagar.com Open

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Bharatpur Development Authority (BDA)?

The Bharatpur Development Authority (भरतपुर विकास प्राधिकरण) is the urban development body for Bharatpur, Rajasthan. This page links to its online services.

How do I log in to a BDA portal?

Open the service you need from the cards above — each portal (PMS, DMS, E-Works, E-Accounts) has its own sign-in page. There is no separate login on this hub page.

Where can I monitor Bharatpur drainage pumps?

Use the Pump Management System (PMS) card above, which opens pms.bdabharatpur.org.

How do I contact the BDA?

Email bdabharatpur@rajasthan.gov.in or visit the official website.
